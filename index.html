02 / Experience

Experiences that shaped how I work

These experiences have strengthened my adaptability, technical problem-solving, and understanding of how people work together.

Experience 01

Learning to live independently

Personal growth

Moving away from my family at 17 to pursue my education taught me how to manage life independently and solve problems without relying on someone else to step in. Over time, this experience made me more confident in my ability to handle challenges and figure out a way forward.

Competency connections: Grit / Resilience / Persistence; Risk Management; Systems Thinking.
This experience required me to adapt, assess situations, and work through unfamiliar challenges.
Experience 02

Studying abroad in Rome and Paris

Cross-cultural learning

During two summers abroad, I studied Thermodynamics and International Marketing in Rome, and Calculus III and Photography in Paris. These experiences let me explore different subjects while learning in unfamiliar environments. One memorable moment in Italy happened when my phone died while I was in an unfamiliar location. I had to explain where I wanted to go and what problem I was having to a restaurant owner who did not speak English.

Competency connections: Communication; Global / Cultural Awareness; Lifelong Learning.
Studying and navigating unfamiliar environments encouraged me to adapt my communication and learn from different perspectives.
Experience 03

Robot Machine Player project

January – May 2026 · Team of five

I worked with a team of five to design, build, and validate a multifunctional robotic system. My responsibilities included leading manufacturing and fabrication, performing calculations involving torque, gear ratios, and drivetrain performance, and developing a complete CAD model and engineering drawings in SolidWorks. One of the most challenging parts of the project was coordinating with my teammates and communicating the reasoning behind our design choices. The experience showed me that successful engineering requires both technical knowledge and the ability to work collaboratively.

Competency connections: Teamwork; Communication; Systems Thinking; Creativity.
The project required coordination, clear explanations of design choices, and attention to how components performed as part of a larger system.
